## Adversarial checks and limits

- Strong L² alone is insufficient; the new branch uses real-zero structure from ES.
- Exponential type and real type are checked before LP membership is used. Arbitrary real-zero entire functions are not substituted.
- The original CK theorem concerns polynomial zeros, not merely critical points. LP approximation on expanding disks supplies the required polynomial sequence.
- Convergence in measure is applied on an interval with a nonzero limit. Weak convergence or a zero limit would not suffice.
- Value normalization is performed only after convergence and nonvanishing at the anchor are established.
- The ordinary projection condition does not construct a sequence satisfying actual ES or capture.
- An energy/gap upper bound is not identified with a necessary capture criterion.
- The earlier ES-independent CMP-R remains valid. No prior historical update is rewritten to assert knowledge of this branch.

This was a fresh source/hypothesis check and a separate reverse-order adversarial pass by the same AI reviewer. It is not independent human peer review or formal verification. Existing general theorems were imported, not re-proved in full.
