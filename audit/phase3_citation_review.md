Primary sources were read through the web tool on the review date. Publisher metadata, an author's manuscript, an abstract and a theorem passage are explicitly different levels of evidence.

Literal link discovery across **232 public Markdown files**, together with the explicit URLs in `data/references.json`, found **402 distinct HTTP(S) URL tokens and 1,201 occurrences**. The inherited bibliography has **111 statement records** and **385 discovery entries**. These numbers are a snapshot, not the count of verified mathematical works. Generated site files, audit files and Git internals were excluded from link discovery. Exact URL aliases such as arXiv PDF/abstract/HTML are not silently combined. URLs outside the selected checks are marked `NOT_CHECKED_IN_THIS_REVIEW`; no HTTP success code is inferred from a web-tool response.

The track pages mostly link to project reports rather than directly to external papers. The citation check therefore followed their critical report/source-note chains. Historical proof-claim records were not promoted to accepted inputs or exhaustively re-audited.

## Nonblocking limitations and follow-up classification

1. **Weil's CERN entry:** [the cited record](https://cds.cern.ch/record/471308) returned a bot-protection page. Its primary contents were not verified in this review. Do not label that specific link as freshly validated. This is an access limitation, not evidence that the citation is false; the precise criterion has other source records.
2. **Resolver access versus identity:** Sauvalle's DOI/arXiv route and the DOI resolver for the Arnoldi paper did not return usable text through this tool. Publisher/author-hosted full texts did, and their headers confirm the bibliographic identities. The Connes–van Suijlekom DOI resolver also failed, but the direct Springer article succeeded. These are not established broken links.
3. **Version pinning:** Burnol v6 is older than the listed v7; it is explicitly cited as v6 and is not called the latest version. Historical notes are not silently upgraded. Cohen–Davenport–Leviatan's old note remains candid about its then-unversioned URL; the new English citation is fixed to v3.
4. **Historical publication states:** records for *Zeta Spectral Triples* include a preprint label and a later published-chapter/review-unconfirmed label. The publisher contents confirm the chapter; they do not prove individual peer review. Preserve the dated statement records rather than turning every publication string into a Boolean. These differences do not alter the missing-step conclusion.
5. **Inventory is not universal validation:** discovery entries and old proof-claim citations are not accepted theorems. The 402-URL list includes unchecked historical and ancillary links. No exhaustive availability, metadata or proof claim is made.

None of these findings requires a guessed title, author, date or DOI replacement. The review found no material conflict between the checked primary statements and the scopes expressed in the current English track pages.
