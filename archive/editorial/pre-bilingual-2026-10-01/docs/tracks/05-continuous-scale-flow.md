# 05. Continuous scale flow and prime return maps

**STATUS: RIEMANN HYPOTHESIS OPEN**

Recorded 2026-09-29. Date of the recorded report; individual start and finish times are not reconstructed.

## Starting idea

Prime periodic orbits have lengths log p. The question was whether their actual arithmetic return maps could explain the real part 1/2 of every zero.

## What was tested

The audit separated a mapping torus, its fiber monodromy, local p-adic transverse action and a genuine smooth Poincaré return map. It also separated local orbit modes from the global zero representation.

## What survived

- The known orbit C_p = ℝ₊×/p^ℤ has length log p. In the chosen lift, the deck convention and the positive-time inverse return convention must be distinguished.

- The p-adic normal action can have valued growth exponent +1 in the stated orientation. Thus a literal claim of no transverse growth is false, without identifying that exponent with a zeta-zero character.

## What failed or remains unproved

Local Haar unitarity does not recover a discrete zeta divisor: the local model has extra modes. A faithful common Hilbert metric on the global arithmetic quotient remains missing. Symplecticity, indefinite pairings and time reversal do not replace it.

## Why the work moved on

The next track asked for the weaker requirement of two-sided subexponential growth of the return operator at p = 2.

## Current scope

Known arithmetic geometry clarified; no new purity theorem or spectral realization claimed.
