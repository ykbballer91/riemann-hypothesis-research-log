# 13. Arithmetic restoring force

**STATUS: RIEMANN HYPOTHESIS OPEN**

Recorded 2026-09-30. Date of the recorded report; individual start and finish times are not reconstructed.

## Starting idea

Could the actual theta kernel impose a force that confines zeros, without simply assuming a global sign condition equivalent to RH?

## What was tested

The track derived off-axis cosine/hyperbolic-sine equations, examined logarithmic derivatives and fixed the de Bruijn–Newman heat sign and time convention. It separated real-axis repulsion from confinement of complex branches.

## What survived

- Known forward strip contraction and the simple-zero evolution law remain valid in their stated domains. Multiple collisions require separate treatment.

- Specific sign information outside a region does not become a sign everywhere. Synthetic heat examples clarify why individual complex zeros need not obey a universal radial inward law.

## What failed or remains unproved

The required global restoring sign returns to an RH-equivalent criterion. Positivity and evenness of the theta kernel do not supply it. A proposed total-positivity property of the actual kernel fails; this is distinct from the Fourier real-zero question.

## Why the work moved on

The next comparison asked whether existing local real-zero theorems could be connected to the actual global approximation family.

## Current scope

Correct evolution laws retained; the time-zero confinement regime remains unproved.
