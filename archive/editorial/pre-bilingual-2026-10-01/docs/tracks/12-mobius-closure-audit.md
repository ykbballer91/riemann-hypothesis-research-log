# 12. Strategy reset: arithmetic estimates and closure

**STATUS: RIEMANN HYPOTHESIS OPEN**

Recorded 2026-09-30. Date of the recorded report; individual start and finish times are not reconstructed.

## Starting idea

This was an inventory rather than a new proof route. It asked whether existing bilinear, short-interval, correlation or causal-closure estimates already supplied the missing pointwise bound.

## What was tested

The audit distinguished pointwise estimates, almost-all statements, logarithmic averages and shift averages. It checked the fixed Gaussian window, sliding derivative energies and the arithmetic delay equations.

## What survived

- Absolute-convergence identities and the unique Möbius inverse in the specified past-decaying class were fixed.

- Local Sobolev control can turn uniform sliding H¹ energy bounds into pointwise bounds. The missing requirement is their quantitative, uniform arithmetic estimate; averages are not categorically incapable of doing this.

## What failed or remains unproved

Known average correlation conclusions do not supply square-root-scale control of every readout. Causal uniqueness does not imply forward stability. Shrinking the Gaussian width with the observation point can make an all-positive sum bounded, so it changes the question instead of proving cancellation.

## Why the work moved on

No next route followed automatically from this inventory. A later, separately requested track tested an arithmetic restoring-force interpretation.

## Current scope

Three proposed mechanisms were not justified. The audit did not claim that all mathematical strategies were exhausted.
