## Technical details

### CORE-S: strict parity forces simplicity

Let $Q_{a,N}$ be the canonical finite Weil matrix, with even and odd restrictions $Q^+$ and $Q^-$. Take $a>0$ and an integer $N\ge1$, and write $e_+$ and $e_-$ for the respective lowest eigenvalues. Then

$$e_+(a,N)<e_-(a,N)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\dim\ker(Q^+-e_+I)=1.$$

Writing $\mathrm{ES}(a,N)$ for a simple, even ground state of the full finite space gives

$$\mathrm{ES}(a,N)\iff e_-(a,N)>e_+(a,N).$$

The formerly independent condition $\Delta_+(a,N)>0$ follows from this strict ordering at each finite point. The case $N=0$, whose odd sector is empty, is excluded here.

### Structure used and short proof

The input is CCM's known matrix structure. In the canonical basis $V_n$ ($-N\le n\le N$), set $DV_n=nV_n$, let $J$ be reflection, and set $\eta=\sum_nV_n$. There is an odd vector $\beta$ such that

$$[D,Q]=|\beta\rangle\langle\eta|-|\eta\rangle\langle\beta|.$$

$$JQ=QJ,\qquad JD=-DJ,\qquad J\eta=\eta,\qquad J\beta=-\beta.$$

$$\ker D=\mathbb C V_0,\qquad\langle\eta,V_0\rangle=1.$$

Let $U$ be the even ground eigenspace and take $\xi\in U$ orthogonal to $\eta$. It is also orthogonal to the odd vector $\beta$. The commutator gives $QD\xi=e_+D\xi$. But $D\xi$ is odd, and strict ordering $e_+<e_-$ excludes that eigenvalue from the odd sector. Hence $D\xi=0$. The kernel and inner-product identities above imply $\xi=0$. Thus the single linear functional $\langle\eta,\cdot\rangle$ is injective on $U$, so $U$ is one-dimensional.

Conversely, a simple even full ground leaves no odd state at the same minimum, giving the equivalence with ES. The argument assumes neither RH nor capture nor a bound on an unknown spectral gap.

### Remaining joint conditions

Let $R_a$ be support restriction, $P_N$ the canonical finite Fourier projection, and $G_{+,j}$ the orthogonal projection onto the entire even ground eigenspace. The recorded theta-kernel normalization is $\widehat k=\Xi/4$. Set

$$C_j=\|(I-G_{+,j})P_{N_j}R_{a_j}k\|_2.$$

The open proposition asks for **one and the same sequence** satisfying

$$a_j\to\infty,\qquad\frac{a_j}{N_j+1}\to0,$$

$$C_j\to0,\qquad e_-(a_j,N_j)>e_+(a_j,N_j)\ \text{eventually}.$$

If these hypotheses hold, CORE-S supplies ES and the adopted ES-plus-strong-L² transfer applies:

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{same-sequence capture}+\text{strict parity}
&\Longrightarrow\mathrm{ES}\\
&\Longrightarrow\text{adopted transfer}\\
&\Longrightarrow\mathrm{RH}.
\end{aligned}$$

Results on unrelated sequences cannot be combined. The earlier CMP-R $\lambda^r$ rate is not added to this branch; CMP-R remains a separate sufficient condition that does not assume ES.
