# 03. The missing Frobenius mechanism

**STATUS: RIEMANN HYPOTHESIS OPEN**

Recorded 2026-09-29. Date of the recorded report; individual start and finish times are not reconstructed.

## Starting idea

Finite-field proofs supply a model for asking where arithmetic origin, trace, duality and purity enter. The aim was to identify the missing theorem over the integers, not to transfer a finite-field analogy by name.

## What was tested

The audit compared the curve/Jacobian polarization argument, broader cohomological purity, Deninger proposals and the existing adelic quotient that represents zeta zeros.

## What survived

- For the finite-field abelian-variety argument, positivity of the Rosati pairing and the relation π†π = q act on the same representation.

- The known adelic test quotient represents the relevant zeros with multiplicities. Its topology and trace statements were distinguished from a positive Hilbert realization.

## What failed or remains unproved

Formal duality and positive integer point counts alone do not establish purity. A natural positive ambient Hilbert norm collapses the intended quotient: its null subspace is dense in that completion. No positive pairing with the required faithful arithmetic descent was supplied.

## Why the work moved on

The work split into an arithmetic-polarization audit and a parallel examination of continuous scaling and prime return maps.

## Current scope

Missing positivity/purity isolated. The historical main state stops here; later tracks have separate states.
