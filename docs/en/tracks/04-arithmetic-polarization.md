# 04. Arithmetic polarization and intersection pairings

**STATUS: RIEMANN HYPOTHESIS OPEN**

Recorded 2026-09-29. Date of the recorded report; individual start and finish times are not reconstructed.

## Starting idea

Could an existing arithmetic intersection or height theorem provide the positive pairing missing from the zero-bearing quotient?

## What was tested

The track compared Arakelov heights, explicit-formula “intersection” language, adelic Fourier involutions and product formulas, checking the actual spaces and subtraction of pole terms.

## What survived

- Known positivity theorems retain their stated geometric domains. The height theory of an arithmetic surface is different from a degree-zero quotient on Spec Z.

- A dense radical obstructs any nonzero closable positive form that is required to vanish there in the specified ambient topology.

## What failed or remains unproved

No exact map identified the established height space with the full zero representation. A natural Fourier-star proposal has negative directions even for actual pole-free tests; this defeats that proposed pairing, not RH or the Weil criterion. Calling an explicit-formula term an intersection does not supply an intersection geometry or a Hodge-index theorem.

## Why the work moved on

The separate scaling track examined the real arithmetic prime orbits directly, then weakened the desired conclusion to a bound for a single prime return.

## Current scope

Existing geometric theorems retained; the required arithmetic polarization was not constructed.
