# 17. Hierarchical selection in fixed derivative families

**STATUS: RIEMANN HYPOTHESIS OPEN**

Recorded 2026-09-30. Date of the recorded report; individual start and finish times are not reconstructed.

## Starting idea

After the leading boundary energy becomes degenerate, do successive terms select k within F_m = span{k,k″,…,k⁽²ᵐ⁾}? The order of operations is the actual projection P_N R_a.

## What was tested

The work derived fixed-m support asymptotics, controlled their Gram matrices, compared Fourier resolution with the boundary scale, and separately audited prime rank-one perturbations and Sonin filtrations.

## What survived

- For every fixed finite m, the support-restricted smallest eigenvalue is eventually simple and its normalized minimizer tends to k/‖k‖. The recorded leading term is a(m!)²Y^(7/2−2m)e^(−2Y)/(√π‖k‖²), with Y = πe^(2a).

- The same restricted selection survives P_N R_a under liminf N/(aY) > 4/π². This is a sufficient resolution condition; necessity or a sharp threshold was not proved.

- The prime-event derivative has a rank-one description, but finite updates need not be positive. The Sonin/Gamma dictionary is exact in its own spaces and does not intertwine the current energy with a replacement selector.

## What failed or remains unproved

Constants and errors are not uniform in growing m. No estimate controls the complementary directions of the full finite matrix. Evenness/simplicity of the relevant full ground states and G* remain missing. Restricted selection is not full ground capture.

## Why the work moved on

A separately dated update asked only whether the union of these derivative families is dense in even L². It did not start the later full-ground steps.

## Current scope

Fixed finite-m hierarchy established within its stated domain; full ground, growing m and RH remain open.
