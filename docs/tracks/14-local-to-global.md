# 14. Local-to-global zero confinement

**STATUS: RIEMANN HYPOTHESIS OPEN**

Recorded 2026-09-30. Date of the recorded report; individual start and finish times are not reconstructed.

## Starting idea

Several local or constrained transforms have real zeros. The task was to identify exactly what must converge to transfer that conclusion to Xi.

## What was tested

The audit compared Hermite–Mellin results, local-field statements, completed transforms with finite correction factors, semilocal Sonin stability and the finite Weil-ground construction.

## What survived

- The hypotheses of the real-zero theorems include their specific vector, positivity, support or orthogonality conditions. A completed transform containing ζ as a factor cannot remove off-line zeta zeros by a holomorphic correction.

- Two families were kept separate: actual finite Weil ground states, whose real-zero theorem needs the stated evenness and simplicity hypothesis ES; and a prolate proxy whose transform converges locally to a scalar multiple of Xi.

## What failed or remains unproved

No theorem identifies the normalized true ground state with that proxy. The missing comparison G* is convergence of their transforms on compact subsets of |Im z| < 1/2, together with the required ground-state hypotheses. The combined criterion is sufficient; an RH converse for G* was not established.

## Why the work moved on

The common-parent track asked whether an actual energy identity, residual estimate or spectral gap could provide this comparison.

## Current scope

Local theorems and proxy convergence retained; the global bridge remains open.
